The Estate

Never shared. Never divided.

Ses Garites is one of the most extraordinary historic estates on the Costa Brava — a 16th-century Catalan masia set within the medieval walls of Pals, moments from the Mediterranean coast.

Original stone vaulted ceilings, exposed wooden beams, and terracotta floors that have stood for over five hundred years offer an unrivalled backdrop for intimate, unforgettable weddings.

The entire estate is available only as a whole. Your celebration, your home, your story.
